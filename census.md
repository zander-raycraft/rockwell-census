# Task - Census Ingestion

## Problem

We get a bunch of (messy) censuses from our clients, which come in a wide variety of formats. We then need to reformat them into a new census format for:

- **A)** vendors, who use it to generate reports
- **B)** carriers, who use it for quoting

There are a number of carriers and vendors, each with their own individually specified formats (some CSV, different encodings for columns, etc)

### More details

The clients send us censuses that include information about the employees, and sometimes about their dependents (though not always).

These censuses can be generated by payroll systems, benadmin systems, or are sometimes kept in bespoke spreadsheets for older clients. The data is messy. Some of them can be fairly long -- a couple thousand rows.

Sometimes the relevant information is spread out over multiple documents -- for instance, one document might have enrollment tiers, and another might have SSNs. To make matters worse, the names don't always match, there can be subtle differences between names, etc. 

Sometimes, the documents contain directly contradicting information (ie same name and date of birth, different SSN), in which case we need to go back to our client's HR to understand why the discrepancy exists.

See the examples for a taste.

### Operational details

We get this data for a "benefit diagnostic" (which we pass to a vendor). This is an approximation we use for sales purposes, however.

## Task

Design a system to ingest, store, reconcile, and export census data. At minimum, it should take the example files, reconcile company B's two files into one census, store it, and be able to export it into two formats of your choosing.

Testing/evaluation of your system (and how you choose to go about that) is particularly important.

## Process

1. **Study the problem.** Use AI to contextualize yourself in the priors of the workflow. Example files are in `examples/` — they're illustrative, you're not expected to handle every case.
2. **Design a specification** (recommend you use AI to refine it) based on what you learn from studying. It doesn't need to be long — 1-2 pages is plenty. Think through what belongs in a backend data storage layer, what should be in LLMs/agents, what might need to surface for human review (and how this is presented), and how/where all this information is stored.
3. **Talk through the spec with me** over a quick 20 minute call.
4. **Build a basic version**. We can down-scope it during our call.
5. **Walk me through** the trade-offs, key decision points, and your thought process on what does and doesn't matter over another 30 minute call.
